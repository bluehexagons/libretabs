# LibreTabs 0.0.1-prototype.9

Evaluation prototype, not the completed lesson-based MVP.

[Play and read the practice guide](https://bluehexagons.github.io/libretabs/).
If an older cached player appears after updating, finish your session and reload.

## What changed

- Every built-in song now has a simple original bass part alongside its melody. These practice basses are new teaching additions, not historical accompaniments.
- The two-hand piano exercise has separate treble and bass MIDI parts. In Songs, choose the part to practice; in Volume & parts, mute either part independently. Each mute choice remains in place when you switch focus.
- Treble focus and Bass focus layouts show the other part on a compact five-line staff. The full piano layout shows both parts together. The optional piano keys show sounding pitches from both visible parts.
- Songs with a pickup still open with the melody as the focused part. The bass enters with the pickup rather than during its padded opening rest.

## Useful checks for testers

- Open several built-in songs, press Play, and check that you hear lower notes along with the melody. Turn the bass off in Volume & parts, then the melody off, and switch the focused part to confirm the mute choices remain independent.
- Choose Treble focus and Bass focus in Quick start or Practice layouts. Check that the smaller staff shows the other part as the score follows playback, including on a phone and a mirrored Theater screen.
- Open the two-hand piano exercise and compare the full two-staff view with each focus layout. Try both reduced motion and ordinary motion.

## Downloads

- Web ZIP: for an HTTPS host or itch.io HTML upload; not a double-click offline app.
- Windows x86_64 ZIP: extract completely and run `LibreTabs.exe`.
- Linux x86_64 ZIP: extract completely and run `./libretabs.x86_64`.

Native builds are unsigned. Download only from this project's release page and
verify `SHA256SUMS`. Do not disable operating-system security protections; report
blocked launches with the OS and message. No Godot installation is required.

## Known limits and feedback

Guitar tabs still mean six-string E-standard guitar. The bass staff has no bass
fingering. The two-part staff pairing follows pitch range, not an imported
file's hand labels. A single part splits at middle C; files with more than two
pitched parts are not automatically reduced to a duet. Procedural audio,
simplified notation, and generated guitar arrangements remain under evaluation.
The six-lesson course is not complete. Imported MIDI is session-only and stays
on the device. Automated and VM browser checks do not establish performance on
every phone, TV, or browser.

Report the version, OS/browser, steps, expected result, and actual result through
https://github.com/bluehexagons/libretabs/issues. Use original or redistributable
MIDI reproductions and remove private filenames and personal details.
