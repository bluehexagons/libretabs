# LibreTabs 0.0.1-prototype.7

Evaluation prototype, not the completed lesson-based MVP.

[Play and read the practice guide](https://bluehexagons.github.io/libretabs/).
The browser player may reload once on its first visit while preparing offline files.

## What changed

- Songs are direct choices, with two simpler melodies first. The current song is named in the chooser. In Theater, Songs is the first Menu item.
- Theater shows more music across large screens while keeping its controls compact. Play tucks the controls; Show controls or F10 brings them back.
- Follow playback keeps nearby music in view and slides between lines. Note cues and page turns are gentler, and reduced motion removes decorative movement.
- The in-app Quick start, reading guidance, and practice guide explain more of the first session.

## Useful checks for testers

- On a phone and a wide screen, change songs, play through the count-in, slow the music, and repeat two measures. Check that the score and sound stay together.
- Try Theater on a desktop, tablet, or mirrored display. Check legibility, control access, and the separate Fullscreen action.
- Try Manual pages and Follow playback, then turn on reduced motion in Appearance & text. Check that the next action stays clear without animation.
- Open a MIDI file, then try both browser players and an offline reload after a complete first download. If you report a problem, share only a redistributable reproduction.

## Downloads

- Web ZIP: for an HTTPS host or itch.io HTML upload; not a double-click offline app.
- Windows x86_64 ZIP: extract completely and run `LibreTabs.exe`.
- Linux x86_64 ZIP: extract completely and run `./libretabs.x86_64`.

Native builds are unsigned. Download only from this project's release page and
verify `SHA256SUMS`. Do not disable operating-system security protections; report
blocked launches with the OS and message. No Godot installation is required.

## Known limits and feedback

Procedural audio, simplified notation, and generated guitar arrangements remain
under evaluation; the six-lesson course is not complete. Imported MIDI is
session-only and stays on the device. Automated and VM browser checks do not
establish performance on every phone, TV, or browser.

Report the version, OS/browser, steps, expected result, and actual result through
https://github.com/bluehexagons/libretabs/issues. Use original or redistributable
MIDI reproductions and remove private filenames and personal details.
