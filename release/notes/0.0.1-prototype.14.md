# LibreTabs 0.0.1-prototype.14

Evaluation build for manual testing. Musical and platform acceptance remain open.

[Open LibreTabs](https://bluehexagons.github.io/libretabs/).
Finish your session and reload after an update. Check the running version in
**Menu → About LibreTabs**.

## Changes since prototype.13

- Short landscape layouts have smaller control groups and a scrollable controls
  column. Play and speed stay prominent; lower options remain accessible by
  touch, wheel or keyboard. Narrow portrait layouts also compact the transport
  when listening is active, including 200% text. Fullscreen remains in Menu.
- The tuner puts **Instrument** and **Target note** first. **Start listening**
  starts it directly; room setup is optional. Device, sensitivity, reference and
  timing options are under **Input settings**, with instructions under Tuner help.
- **Use microphone in practice** starts/resumes listening and enables feedback
  together. It returns to the score after a successful connection; errors stay
  visible. Pause/resume icons are available beside the player while connected.
- **Mute song notes** silences musical sound and its echoes, keeping the
  metronome and count-in audible. Saved volumes and playback time are preserved.
- The default background is flat with a subtle woven texture. Optional shades
  and control colors are quieter. More actions have SVG icons.
- The Pages site now starts with player/download links and compact help sections,
  using the app's colors, icons and textures.

## Manual checks

- Rotate a phone to landscape with listening active. Test both control sides,
  fullscreen, 200% text and a visible browser toolbar. Scroll to every control.
  Check that dragging from a button does not activate it.
- Choose the appropriate instrument, then Start listening. Test pause/resume,
  denied permission, device changes and Use microphone in practice.
- Mute song notes during playback. Confirm music and keyboard previews are quiet
  while the metronome/count-in still sound; unmute without losing your position.
- Use an electronic piano at a comfortable speaker volume. Test note changes,
  silence and low/center/high needle indications. Use headphones to isolate clicks.
- Read Input settings and Tuner help only when needed. Check Light/Dark/Midnight,
  large text, keyboard focus and the revised Pages help on a phone.

## Downloads and known limits

Extract native ZIPs completely, then run LibreTabs.exe on Windows or
./libretabs.x86_64 on Linux. Native packages are unsigned; verify SHA256SUMS.
The web ZIP requires HTTPS hosting. Browser offline use requires a completed
first download and retained site storage.

Microphone listening estimates one note at a time and cannot assess chords.
Mute changes fade over at most 20 ms plus queued audio/device latency. Keeping
clicks audible can let them reach a microphone; no echo cancellation is provided.
Notation, guitar placement and procedural audio remain prototype projections.
Physical phone/piano/microphone, Windows, Firefox/Safari and independent timing
acceptance still require testing. The compatibility player has weaker timing
expectations and stalled audio in prior T3 tests; prefer the main player or native app.

Report version, device/browser, input/audio setup and reproduction steps at
https://github.com/bluehexagons/libretabs/issues.
