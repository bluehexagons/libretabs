Start practicing

  1. Listen. On a fresh launch, Ode to Joy is ready. Choose Start at half speed in Quick start, then press Play. The clicks before the music are the count-in, if enabled.
  2. Follow a few notes. In the guitar layout, try 0–0–1–3 on the top tab line. Pluck the open string twice, then press behind the first and third frets.
  3. Repeat one measure. Pause and tap the opening music. Choose Repeat → Repeat this measure, then press Play. A measure is the group of beats between vertical lines.

In Quick start, Repeat this measure sets up the same loop.

If the opening has scrolled away, choose Repeat → Choose a range. Set From and Through to 1, then choose Play selected section.

For another tune, find Hot Cross Buns in Songs and try the first two bars. Open About for practice cues, history and sources.

More guitar and piano exercises

Read the music

Guitar tabs use six lines for the strings. The top line is the thinnest string. Numbers tell you which fret, or metal strip, to press behind. 0 means pluck the open string without pressing. Standard tuning is E–A–D–G–B–E, thickest to thinnest.

Read left to right. With the default highlights, outlines mark sounding tab numbers and curved underlines show the next notes. Menu → Help & app → Help explains the rhythm and staff symbols.

When you can follow a section steadily, toggle My part to play the melody yourself while any backing and beat clicks continue. Turn it back on to hear the melody again.

After browsing away from playback, press Back to current note to return to the current position. This keeps playback playing or paused.

Tuner & microphone

  1. Open Tuner & listening and choose your Instrument.
  2. Press Start listening and allow microphone access.
  3. Sound one clear note. Center the needle to match the nearest note or your selected Target note.

Turn on Show notes on score, then close the tuner to see notes as you play. The on-screen keyboard has a separate setting. Mute song notes silences the app’s musical sound while keeping the metronome and count-in audible. Headphones help keep clicks out of the microphone.

For electric bass, choose Menu → Practice → Practice layouts → Electric bass for four-string tab and a bass staff. It selects the lower part on a two-part song. Plug into an audio interface and select it in Tuner → Settings before starting to listen.

Pause or resume listening from the player. Device, sensitivity, room setup and pitch reference are under Settings. Setup is optional. Microphone listening detects one note at a time; chords and sustained notes can confuse it.

Player controls

Music lines switches between smooth scrolling and pages. Fewer lines make each line taller. Note spacing controls how much music fits across.

Menu → Screen & interface → Appearance & text changes text size, appearance and control placement. On short landscape screens, swipe or scroll the controls column to reach every option.

Practice layouts includes six-string guitar tabs, four-string bass tabs and piano staffs. Treble focus and Bass focus give the chosen part more room.

Space plays or pauses with menus closed. F9 switches Theater view.

Import & print

Songs → Import & exercises → Import MIDI opens a .mid or .midi file from your device. MIDI describes notes and timing; recordings, PDFs and photos cannot be opened as songs. Formats 0 and 1 are supported, up to 256 KiB with further song limits.

Select the part to practice. In Volume & instruments → Parts to hear, switch song parts on or off. Generated tabs can omit notes that cannot be placed; review About this arrangement.

Print your music saves a song-named HTML file. Open it in a browser to print or save as PDF. Capture & overlay provides a score view for separate recording software.

Help & feedback

No sound

Tap Play to allow browser audio and wait for the count-in, if enabled. Turn off Mute song notes, check device volume, then check Instrument volume and enabled parts under Menu → Sound & input → Volume & instruments.

Player will not start

If Browser startup offers a compatibility player, try it. Windows and Linux downloads are also available.

An older version is still showing

Finish your session, reconnect, then reload. Imported music must be reopened. Avoid clearing site data first: it removes saved preferences and offline files.
