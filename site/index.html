Start practicing

  1. Choose a practice layout in Quick start, then Start practicing.
  2. Press Play to hear the ready exercise. In Songs, Listen previews a tune and Try opens it.
  3. Lower the speed to play along. 50% is half speed with the same pitches.

Loop repeats a range of measures—groups of beats between vertical lines. Set From and Through, then turn the loop on.

Read the music

Guitar tabs use six lines for the strings. The top line is the thinnest string. Numbers are frets, not fingers; 0 means an open string. Standard tuning is E–A–D–G–B–E, thickest to thinnest.

Read left to right. Sounding notes become bolder; curved underlines show the next notes. Menu → Help explains the rhythm and staff symbols.

After browsing away from playback, press Back to current note to return to the current position. This keeps playback playing or paused.

Tuner & microphone

  1. Open Tuner & listening and choose your Instrument.
  2. Press Start listening and allow microphone access.
  3. Sound one clear note. Center the needle to match the nearest note or your selected Target note.

Use microphone in practice starts listening and enables pitch feedback. Mute song notes silences the app’s musical sound while keeping the metronome and count-in audible. Headphones help keep clicks out of the microphone.

Pause or resume listening from the player. Device, sensitivity, room setup and pitch reference are under Input settings. Setup is optional. Microphone listening detects one note at a time; chords and sustained notes can confuse it.

Player controls

Music lines switches between smooth scrolling and pages. Fewer lines make each line taller. Note spacing controls how much music fits across.

Menu → Display changes text size, appearance and control placement. On short landscape screens, swipe or scroll the controls column to reach every option.

Practice layouts includes guitar tabs, piano staffs and bass-staff reading. Bass staffs show sounding pitches; the six-string tabs are guitar fingering.

Space plays or pauses with menus closed. F9 switches Theater view.

Import & print

Songs → Import MIDI opens a .mid or .midi file from your device. MIDI describes notes and timing; recordings, PDFs and photos cannot be opened as songs. Formats 0 and 1 are supported, up to 256 KiB with further song limits.

Select the part to practice. In Volume & parts, choose which parts to hear. Generated guitar placements can omit crowded chord tones; review About this arrangement.

Print your music saves a song-named HTML file. Open it in a browser to print or save as PDF. Capture & overlay provides a score view for separate recording software.

Help & feedback

No sound

Tap Play to allow browser audio. Turn off Mute song notes, check device volume, then check Instrument volume and enabled parts under Volume & parts. Speed must be above 0%.

Player will not start

Try the compatibility player from Browser startup above. Its timing and audio response may be less consistent. Native downloads offer another option.

An older version is still showing

Finish your session, reconnect, then reload. Imported music must be reopened. Avoid clearing site data first: it removes saved preferences and offline files.
